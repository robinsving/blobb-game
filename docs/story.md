# Blobb: story bible

## The frame (never stated outright)

Two narrators are telling Blobb's story. They are an older person and a child, in a hospital at night. The player is not told this; it is revealed only gradually.

**Rules for all dialogue**
- **No family words.** No mum, dad, son, daughter, grandma, "honey" and the like. The relationship must be felt, not named: one voice is older, calmer, a little dry and wise (**O**, serif text, left); the other is younger, curious, insistent and naive (**Y**, hand-written text, right).
- **Y has authority over the story.** When Y objects, the world changes: Blobb's colour, the style of the scene, monsters, branches, physics. O gently goes along with it. This is the core mechanic.
- **Blobb** is "he" / "han". He is green; this was settled in ch1, where O said purple and Y corrected it.
- Every line exists in **English and Swedish** (`Ln.o(en, sv)`).
- **Tone arc.** Light-hearted and funny at first. Later chapters move toward childlike questions about what happens when we die. Hospital hints grow chapter by chapter: ch1 has only a single quiet monitor beep at the very end, plus "it's very late" and "your eyes are saying something else".
- **Failure is a retell.** Y says "that's not what happened!" and the scene rewinds.

## Chapters

1. **The Other Side of the Forest** (done). Side-view forest platformer, then a top-down box-pushing puzzle in a clearing ("like a bird sees it").
   Beats: "Tell me a story" → O doesn't know any stories → O notices the drawing of Blobb on the wall → "only if you help me, I don't know everything Blobb did" (Y becomes co-narrator) → Blobb appears on blank paper → purple/green ("You see? I need your help already") → a dark, realistic forest becomes a crayon forest → "Blobb could jump super high" (jump unlocked) → pits with retells → a giant grumpy banana that Y shrinks → "the tree grew branches" → evening turns everything orange → "like a bird" fly-up into the top-down view → Blobb as a "fuzzy pancake" → crates and gate → a stream where "wooden crates float" (they become bridges) → Blobb rests → "Tomorrow, Blobb learns to swim. I promise." → beep.
2. **Swimming.** An underwater, floaty swim in a pool, then a puzzle based on something humorous. Pays off the ch1 promise ("Can Blobb swim?" "Not yet.").
3. **The City.** A night city with street lamps: "the lights from the street lamps enhancing his yellow glow" makes Blobb yellow, then Y asks "Wait, wasn't he green?" and he turns green. Then a rooftop chase escaping birds, timed to rapid beeps from a hospital machine. The hospital's lit sign is visible in the distance. Fade to white, with O screaming "Help".
4. **Quiet.** Y: "Can we talk about a day when Blobb had less chasing, and more quiet?" A quiet, minimal climb up a mountain that ends under a starry sky. Y: "What do you think happens when we die?" O: "..."

## Style per scene

Mix styles freely, and let the narrators switch them: realistic, child's drawing, monochrome and so on. Backgrounds can change mid-scene when a narrator says so.
