BLOBB: LICENSES AND CREDITS
===========================

Original content
----------------
The story, the code, the drawings Blobb is based on, the animations, the voice in
the jump sound, and all music and sound effects (synthesised by the game's own
scripts in tools/) were made for this game.

Generated art in assets/gen/ was made for this game with image generation
(Stable Diffusion XL in ComfyUI) or drawn in code.
