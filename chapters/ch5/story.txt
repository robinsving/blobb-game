# =====================================================================
#  BLOBB, Chapter 5: The Climb
# =====================================================================
#
#  Format: see chapters/ch1/story.txt.
#
#  The last chapter. Up the mountain from where ch4 ended, from night
#  through blue hour to sunrise. It ends hopeful: O recovers.
#
#  O COMES BACK, FAINT. At the foot O only manages fragments and Y fills
#  the gaps (often with O's own words). The lines grow as Blobb climbs;
#  the dry humour returns; halfway up O makes a rule of O's own for the
#  first time. By the top they're equals again, half each, which is
#  exactly what failed in ch4. Lighter again: jokes and playful retells
#  come back, from both voices now.
#
#  No family words: ch4's "...Dad?" was the one break. They're back to
#  being two story-tellers. No caretaking, no reassurance, no permission.
#
#  Mechanics (docs/story.md):
#    heartbeat ledges  on each monitor beat some ledges kick up like a
#                      springboard and launch Blobb (no timing needed). The
#                      beat is uneven at the foot and steadies as O recovers.
#    stretch & swing   glowing holds (roots, pegs; stars near the top).
#                      Near one, press a button: Blobb stretches to it and
#                      swings; let go to fling. Unlocked by O's rule.
#
#  Scene names must match the level code (chapters/ch5/climb.gd: the
#  TRIGGERS table is by height; "swing" plays after the first swing,
#  "summit" on the top, "room" after it, fail_1..4 on a long fall).
# =====================================================================


# ---------------------------------------------------------------------
#  PART 1: THE FOOT  (night; the beat uneven; O in fragments)
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------

# TRIGGER: the chapter starts on the ledges where ch4 ended. Night, the
# slow, even beep from ch4's last seconds. Blocking: this is O's return.
=== intro ===
@pause 2.0
O: ...Where...
  sv: ...Var...
@pause 2.0
O: ...had...
  sv: ...var vi...
Y: ...had we got to. That's your line. You're back.
  sv: ...någonstans. Det är din replik. Du är tillbaka.
@pause 1.5
O: Was I... away?
  sv: Var jag... borta?
Y: A bit. Blobb waited.
  sv: Lite grann. Blobb väntade.
O: ...Good of him.
  sv: ...Hyggligt av honom.
@pause 1.0
O: And he was... where?
  sv: Och han var... var?
Y: At the foot of the mountain. Where you left him.
  sv: Vid foten av berget. Där du lämnade honom.
O: A mountain. Yes. ...Tall. And very dark.
  sv: Ett berg. Ja. ...Högt. Och väldigt mörkt.
  @mountain
Y: Not so dark. My half: stars. All of them.
  sv: Inte så mörkt. Min halva: stjärnor. Allihop.
  @stars
O: ...All of them. There.
  sv: ...Allihop. Så där.
Y: Better.
  sv: Bättre.


# TRIGGER: the first heartbeat ledge (a few steps in). It kicks on each
# beat, unevenly.
=== heartbeat (ambient) ===
Y: Listen. The mountain has a heartbeat.
  sv: Lyssna. Berget har hjärtslag.
@pause 1.5
O: ...An uneven one.
  sv: ...Ett ojämnt.
Y: Then stand on it and wait for the beat.
  sv: Då ställer man sig på den och väntar på slaget.


# TRIGGER: a patch of moss on a ledge (the same glowing moss as ch4).
=== moss (ambient) ===
O: Once... in the days when...
  sv: En gång... på den tiden när...
@pause 2.0
Y: ...when the moss was still young. I know it now.
  sv: ...när mossan fortfarande var ung. Jag kan den nu.
O: You do it better.
  sv: Du gör det bättre.
Y: I don't. I just remembered it.
  sv: Nix. Jag kom bara ihåg den.


# TRIGGER: higher up the foot. O's first joke: the ch1 colour mix-up,
# on purpose this time.
=== colour (ambient) ===
O: Blobb climbed on, yellow as a...
  sv: Blobb klättrade vidare, gul som en...
Y: GREEN.
  sv: GRÖN.
O: ...Green. Just checking you were listening.
  sv: ...Grön. Jag ville bara se om du lyssnade.
Y: You weren't checking.
  sv: Det ville du inte alls.
@pause 1.0
O: No.
  sv: Nej.


# ---------------------------------------------------------------------
#  PART 2: THE MIDDLE  (blue hour; the beat steadier; O's rule)
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------

# TRIGGER: a ledge too high to reach, even off a heartbeat ledge on the
# beat. A glowing root hangs above it. Blocking: O's first rule.
=== rule ===
Y: Too high. Even on the beat.
  sv: För högt. Även i takt.
O: Then... in my half...
  sv: Då... i min halva...
@pause 1.5
O: In my half, Blobb is a good deal longer than he looks.
  sv: I min halva är Blobb betydligt längre än han ser ut.
  @stretch
Y: That's a rule. You made a RULE.
  sv: Det där är en regel. Du hittade på en REGEL.
O: Somebody has to, now and then.
  sv: Någon måste ju, då och då.
Y: That's MY job.
  sv: Det är MITT jobb.
O: Half each.
  sv: Hälften var.


# TRIGGER: the first swing (Blobb lets go of the first hold).
=== swing (ambient) ===
Y: He's like a sock with a stone in it.
  sv: Han är som en strumpa med en sten i.
O: A very determined sock.
  sv: En mycket beslutsam strumpa.


# TRIGGER: the end of the middle stretch. The beat is regular now.
=== steady (ambient) ===
Y: It's beating properly now. Hear it?
  sv: Nu slår det ordentligt. Hör du?
@pause 1.0
O: I hear it.
  sv: Jag hör.


# ---------------------------------------------------------------------
#  PART 3: THE HIGH STRETCH  (the last stars; equals again)
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------

# TRIGGER: the high stretch starts. The holds up here are low stars.
=== stars (ambient) ===
O: Up here the stars hang so low you could hang a coat on one.
  sv: Här uppe hänger stjärnorna så lågt att man kunde hänga en rock på dem.
Y: Blobb hasn't got a coat.
  sv: Blobb har ingen rock.
O: Then he'll have to swing from one instead.
  sv: Då får han väl svinga sig i en i stället.


# TRIGGER: the pigeons from ch3 fly up alongside (friendly now; they
# keep pace a while, then peel away toward the top).
=== pigeons (ambient) ===
Y: The pigeons! ...They're not chasing him.
  sv: Duvorna! ...De jagar honom inte.
O: They never were. They were keeping up.
  sv: Det gjorde de aldrig. De höll bara jämna steg.
Y: In my half they're showing him the way.
  sv: I min halva visar de honom vägen.
O: In mine they're arguing about it.
  sv: I min grälar de om den.


# TRIGGER: near the top, the last stars going out one by one.
# The softened question (the director's decision). Long gaps; don't fill them.
=== question (ambient) ===
Y: Where do stories go when they end?
  sv: Vart tar sagor vägen när de tar slut?
@pause 2.5
O: Up. Into the air. Everywhere, a little.
  sv: Upp. Ut i luften. Lite överallt.
Y: That's what you said about bubbles.
  sv: Det sa du om bubblor.
O: It's true of most things.
  sv: Det stämmer på det mesta.
@pause 2.0
Y: In my half, this one doesn't end yet.
  sv: I min halva tar den här inte slut än.
O: ...In mine it goes on a good while.
  sv: ...I min fortsätter den en bra stund till.
Y: Good. That's both halves.
  sv: Bra. Då är det båda halvorna.


# ---------------------------------------------------------------------
#  PART 4: THE SUMMIT, AND THE ROOM
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------

# TRIGGER: Blobb stands on the summit. Deep blue, one star left.
# Blocking. Pays off ch4, where "In my half, it's morning." did nothing:
# a rule only works with both halves.
=== summit ===
@pause 2.0
Y: In my half, it's morning.
  sv: I min halva är det morgon.
@pause 2.5
O: ...In mine too.
  sv: ...I min också.
  @sunrise
  @hold 3.0
Y: It worked.
  sv: Det funkade.
O: It took both halves.
  sv: Det krävdes båda halvorna.
Y: It always does.
  sv: Det gör det alltid.


# TRIGGER: after the sunrise, a slow cross-fade to the real room in
# morning light: two silhouettes, the window, and the drawing of Blobb
# on the wall (assets/drawing.jpeg). The steady beep, quieter. Ambient,
# short; then the end card.
=== room (ambient) ===
@pause 3.0
O: Where had we got to?
  sv: Var var vi någonstans?
@pause 2.0
Y: ...The other side of the forest.
  sv: ...Andra sidan skogen.
  @hold 3.0


# ---------------------------------------------------------------------
#  RETELLS (Blobb falls off the mountain; playful, from both voices now)
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------

# TRIGGER: falls 1-4, in turn.
=== fail_1 (ambient) ===
Y: He didn't fall!
  sv: Han ramlade inte!
O: He didn't. I'd remember.
  sv: Nej. Det skulle jag minnas.
Y: You'd forget.
  sv: Du skulle glömma.
O: Not that.
  sv: Inte det.

=== fail_2 (ambient) ===
O: And then Blobb fell all the way down to...
  sv: Och sen föll Blobb hela vägen ner till...
Y: Nope.
  sv: Nix.
O: ...to a slightly lower ledge. And climbed straight back up.
  sv: ...till en lite lägre avsats. Och klättrade genast upp igen.
Y: Better.
  sv: Bättre.

=== fail_3 (ambient) ===
Y: That was the mountain's fault.
  sv: Det var bergets fel.
O: Mountains have a great deal to answer for.
  sv: Berg har mycket att stå till svars för.

=== fail_4 (ambient) ===
O: Again. From the last good bit.
  sv: Igen. Från senaste bra biten.
Y: The last good bit was ALL of it.
  sv: Senaste bra biten var ALLTIHOP.


# =====================================================================
#  Effects used in this chapter (what each @effect does)
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------
#  @mountain      (intro) the dark lifts halfway: the mountain, dimly
#  @stars         (intro) the rest of the dark lifts and the stars come out
#  @stretch       stretch & swing unlocked (the holds wake up; hint)
#  @sunrise       the sun comes up over the summit; the sky to morning
#  After "room", the level shows the end card and goes to the title.
#  The beat itself (uneven at the foot, steadier at "steady", even at the
#  top) is driven by height climbed, not by lines.
# =====================================================================
