# =====================================================================
#  BLOBB, Chapter 4: The Other Half
# =====================================================================
#
#  Format: see chapters/ch1/story.txt.
#
#  A short dreamscape. No puzzles, no danger, no retells: Blobb only walks,
#  on a path that keeps quietly looping, through a soft world that never
#  settles on one style. Bits of the earlier chapters drift past.
#
#  ONLY Y SPEAKS. O never answers; every gap Y leaves is O's half, empty.
#  For the first time Y's rules do nothing: the story only works with both
#  halves. Y slowly stops talking about Blobb and starts talking to O.
#  The family word at the very end is the one place the game breaks the
#  "no family words" rule (the director's decision; see docs/story.md).
#
#  Ending: "...Dad?" / "Wake up." Then the mountain, and a short, simple
#  climb: this is O climbing back to consciousness. The monitor beep comes
#  back as Blobb climbs, faint and uneven at the foot, steadier with every
#  ledge, slow and normal at the top. (ch5 is the real climb, later.)
#
#  Scene names must match the level code (not built yet):
#    dream: chapters/ch4/dream.gd   (TRIGGERS table = distance walked)
# =====================================================================


# TRIGGER: the chapter starts, out of ch3's white. The white thins into
# a pale, soft world. Blobb is already standing there. No O opening line.
=== intro (ambient) ===
@pause 2.0
Y: ...Hello?
  sv: ...Hallå?
@pause 2.5
Y: Where had we got to?
  sv: Var var vi någonstans?
@pause 2.0
Y: That's your line. You always say that.
  sv: Det ska du säga. Det säger du alltid.

# TRIGGER: Blobb has walked a little way.
=== roofs (ambient) ===
Y: Blobb was running. On the roofs. And then...
  sv: Blobb sprang. På taken. Och sen...
@pause 2.5
Y: I don't know the next bit. That's your half.
  sv: Jag kan inte fortsättningen. Det är din halva.

# TRIGGER: a patch of moss drifts past, glowing a little.
=== moss (ambient) ===
Y: Once, in the days when the moss was still young...
  sv: En gång, på den tiden när mossan fortfarande var ung...
@pause 2.0
Y: That's how you start it. You do it better.
  sv: Så brukar du börja. Du gör det bättre.

# TRIGGER: Y tries a rule. NOTHING happens: no change of light, no effect.
# The silence after it matters; don't fill it.
=== morning (ambient) ===
Y: In my half, it's morning.
  sv: I min halva är det morgon.
@pause 2.5
Y: In my half, it's MORNING.
  sv: I min halva är det MORGON.
@pause 2.0
Y: If it's morning, you must wake up.
  sv: Om det är morgon måste du vakna.
@pause 3.0
Y: ...It's not working.
  sv: ...Det funkar inte.

# TRIGGER: the rubber duck floats by in mid-air, still cross.
=== duck (ambient) ===
Y: The duck. Still cross.
  sv: Ankan. Fortfarande arg.
@pause 1.5
Y: ...That's what you say. "Still cross."
  sv: ...Det är ju du som brukar säga så. "Fortfarande arg."

# TRIGGER: a street lamp from ch3 stands alone on the path, lit.
=== lamp (ambient) ===
Y: I left the lights on. For you.
  sv: Jag lät lamporna vara tända. För din skull.

# TRIGGER: the path has quietly looped; the same stump/lamp comes round again.
=== again (ambient) ===
Y: We've been here before.
  sv: Här har vi varit förut.
@pause 2.0
Y: Blobb's just walking. He doesn't know where. You have to tell him.
  sv: Blobb bara går. Han vet inte vart. Du måste berätta för honom.

# TRIGGER: the last stretch. The world goes very quiet and plain.
# Short lines, long gaps.
=== ending ===
@pause 2.5
Y: ...Dad?
  sv: ...pappa?
@pause 3.0
Y: Dad, wake up.
  sv: Pappa, vakna.
  @hold 2.0
  @mountain

# At the foot of the mountain (dream.gd _clamber): Blobb tries to haul himself up the first
# ledge, slowly, and slides back down. After this line the beep comes back, and with it the
# strength to get up the ledges (the "beep" scene).
=== foot (ambient) ===
Y: Blobb can't manage without you.
  sv: Blobb kan inte klara sig utan dig.

# The first beep: it goes through Blobb (he lights up), and from now on his jump reaches the
# ledges. Y hears it too.
=== beep (ambient) ===
@pause 0.6
Y: Everything's easier when you're with us.
  sv: Allting blir enklare när du är med.

# At the top of the short climb nobody speaks: a few seconds of the slow,
# even beep (the answer), then straight on into chapter 5. No scene needed.


# =====================================================================
#  Effects used in this chapter (what each @effect does)
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------
#  @mountain      the mist ahead thins: a mountain, and a short climb up its
#                 first ledges (simple jumps, no falling off, no retells).
#                 The beep comes back as Blobb climbs, faint and uneven at
#                 the foot and steadier with every ledge, until it's slow and
#                 normal at the top. It keeps going into chapter 5.
# =====================================================================
