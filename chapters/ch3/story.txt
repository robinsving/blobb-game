# =====================================================================
#  BLOBB, Chapter 3: Lights
# =====================================================================
#
#  Same format as chapters/ch1/story.txt (see its header):
#   === name ===   scene (blocking)      === name (ambient) ===   player keeps control
#   O: / Y:        older / younger narrator     sv: optional Swedish line
#     @effect      runs fx_effect() on the level after the line above has typed
#     @hold 0.6    extra wait after a line      @pause 0.8   silence before the next line
#
#  Rules (docs/story.md): equals building a story half each, never parent/child. No family
#  words, no bedtime or caretaker talk, nobody asks permission, reassures or grants. Never
#  mention drawing, crayons, paper or pencils. Blobb is "he".
#  This chapter is where the frame starts to show. O drifts (forgets Blobb's colour), then
#  goes silent for the whole rooftop chase: Y talks alone, keeps leaving gaps for O's half,
#  and finally calls for help. The beat under the chase turns into a monitor beep.
#
#  Scene names must match the level code:
#    city:  chapters/ch3/city.gd    (TRIGGERS table = tile columns)
#    roofs: chapters/ch3/roofs.gd   (the light-bridge rooms)
#    chase: chapters/ch3/chase.gd   (LINES table = beats)
# =====================================================================


# ---------------------------------------------------------------------
#  PART 1: THE TOWN AT NIGHT (side view)
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------

# TRIGGER: the chapter starts, over the poster. Then the street fades in at dusk.
=== intro ===
O: Now. Where had we got to?
Y: Lights. Lots of them. And you were bringing the night.
O: So I was. The river carried Blobb all the way into town, and the sun went down behind the roofs.
  @nightfall
O: There. One night, as promised. Deep and blue, with the stars put in.
Y: Too dark. My half: lights.
  @lamps_on
Y: There. And there. And THERE.
O: One by one, all the way down the street.
# (hint appears: walk)


# TRIGGER: Blobb walks into the light of the first lamps (column 12).
# O's crack: the colour slips. Settled in ch1 ("He's green!"), now it's forgotten.
=== glow ===
O: Under the lamps, Blobb's fuzz shone like honey. Gold, all over.
  @blobb_yellow
Y: Wait. Wasn't he green?
@pause 1.4
O: ...Was he?
Y: Green. We said. Ages ago.
O: Green. Of course he was. I don't know where yellow came from.
  @blobb_green
Y: Green, but shiny. Both.
  @blobb_shine


# TRIGGER: the sweeper's corner (column 30).
=== sweeper (ambient) ===
O: Halfway down the street, someone was sweeping. Someone small, and yellow, and cross.
Y: The banana! He's got ANOTHER job!
O: "No loitering," said the banana.
Y: He's not loitering. He's walking.


# TRIGGER: the banana's broom catches Blobb for the first time.
=== banana_bump (ambient) ===
Y: Hey! Sweep round him!


# TRIGGER: the open stretch before the canal (column 46).
=== dash ===
Y: Then Blobb ran. So fast he went all blurry.
  @dash
O: A green streak, all down the high street.
# (hint appears: run and jump the canal)


# TRIGGER: the pigeons on the shop sign (column 66).
=== pigeons (ambient) ===
Y: Pigeons. Asleep.
O: Pigeons sleep standing up, with one eye open.
Y: Shh. Don't wake them.


# TRIGGER: Blobb falls into the canal. The scene rewinds to the last checkpoint.
# The game cycles fail_1 to fail_4 here and on the roofs.
=== fail_1 ===
Y: Nope. He didn't fall.
O: Didn't he?
Y: He jumped further. Much further.

=== fail_2 ===
O: And down he went...
Y: Up. He went UP. Again.

=== fail_3 ===
Y: Again!
O: Again.

=== fail_4 ===
Y: Blobbs don't fall. They bounce.
O: They bounce. I'll try to keep that in mind.


# TRIGGER: the drainpipe at the end of the street (column 84). Then up to the roofs.
=== climb ===
O: At the end of the street, an old drainpipe went all the way up to the roofs.
Y: Up! Lights from up high, that was the deal.
  @climb


# ---------------------------------------------------------------------
#  PART 2: THE ROOFTOPS (light is solid)
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------

# TRIGGER: the roofs scene starts (room 1).
=== roofs ===
O: Up on the roofs, the whole town was lit up below.
Y: Told you. Lights from up high.
O: But the roofs had gaps between them. Too wide, even for a super jump.
Y: Then the lights help. In my half, light is solid. You can walk on it.
  @solid_light
O: Solid light. Mind your step on it, then.
# (hint appears: cranks turn the lamps)


# TRIGGER: room 2 starts.
=== room2 ===
O: The next roof had two lamps, and they didn't agree about anything.
Y: Stand on one. Turn the other.


# TRIGGER: room 3 starts.
=== room3 ===
O: The last roof. Chimneys in the way, and a long way down.
Y: Light goes through chimneys.
O: It doesn't, you know.
Y: ...Fine. Climb them, then.


# TRIGGER: the player restarts a roof (R) for the first time.
=== restart (ambient) ===
Y: That roof again.
O: From the top.


# TRIGGER: room 3 is solved. O's last line breaks off; Y finishes it alone.
# From here on, O says nothing.
=== birds ===
Y: Look. All of it. Every light in the whole town.
O: And up on the roofs, the birds were...
@pause 2.2
Y: The birds were what?
@pause 1.6
Y: ...Waking up. The birds were waking up.
  @birds_wake
Y: And they were ANGRY.


# ---------------------------------------------------------------------
#  PART 3: THE CHASE (isometric rooftops, on the beat)
#  Only Y speaks. Lines fire at beats (chase.gd LINES). The beat slowly
#  becomes a monitor beep, then speeds up.
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------

=== chase_go (ambient) ===
Y: Run, Blobb! Jump on the beat!

=== chase_1 (ambient) ===
Y: He's faster than the birds. They're SO slow.

=== chase_2 (ambient) ===
Y: Your turn.
@pause 1.6
Y: ...Your half!

=== chase_3 (ambient) ===
Y: You're doing the quiet thing again.

=== chase_4 (ambient) ===
Y: Say the birds are slow. Say it.

=== chase_5 (ambient) ===
Y: What's that noise?

=== chase_6 (ambient) ===
Y: What is going on?

=== chase_7 (ambient) ===
Y: Why is it so fast?

# TRIGGER: Blobb is hit (bird, chimney, a missed gap); he blinks and runs on. Nobody answers now.
=== chase_fail_1 (ambient) ===
Y: He didn't fall. He didn't.

=== chase_fail_2 (ambient) ===
Y: Again. ...Again.

=== chase_fail_3 (ambient) ===
Y: Not like that. Tell it right.

# TRIGGER: the last roof. The tall building with the lit sign fills the view.
# Silence after this: the beeps stop, a slow fade to white, then the chapter card.
=== help ===
Y: ...Help.
@pause 1.2
Y: HELP!
  @white_out


# =====================================================================
#  Effects used in this chapter (what each @effect does)
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------
#  @nightfall      dusk turns to deep blue night
#  @lamps_on       the street lamps come on one by one
#  @blobb_yellow   Blobb turns gold in the lamplight
#  @blobb_green    Blobb goes back to green
#  @blobb_shine    green, with a soft glow near the lamps
#  @dash           Blobb runs blurry-fast for a while (the dash form from the original drawing)
#  @climb          Blobb climbs the drainpipe; on to the roofs
#  @solid_light    lamp beams become solid (walkable)
#  @birds_wake     the pigeons wake and take off; on to the chase
#  @white_out      slow fade to white, the beeps stop
# =====================================================================
