# =====================================================================
#  BLOBB, Chapter 1: The Other Side of the Forest
# =====================================================================
#
#  How to read this file
#  ---------------------
#   === name ===          a scene. The comment above it says what triggers it.
#   === name (ambient) === the player keeps control; lines advance by themselves.
#                         Otherwise the game pauses and the player presses
#                         Space/Enter/(A) to continue.
#   O:                    the older narrator (slow, wistful, old-fashioned images,
#                         a little forgetful; serif text + crescent moon, left)
#   Y:                    the younger narrator (quick, bossy, silly, makes the rules;
#                         hand-written text + star, right)
#     sv:                 Swedish version of the line above. Every line needs one.
#     @effect             something happens in the world when the line above has
#                         finished typing (effect list at the bottom of this file)
#     @hold 0.6           wait a little extra after the line above
#   @pause 0.8            silence before the next line
#   #                     comments and stage directions; the game ignores them
#
#  Rules: they are equals building a story together, never parent/child.
#  No family words, no bedtime/caretaker talk (late, sleep, tired, "your eyes",
#  permission). Never hint WHICH of them is unwell. Blobb is "he"/"han".
#  See docs/story.md for the bigger picture.
#
#  Scene names must match the level code:
#    forest:   chapters/ch1/forest.gd   (TRIGGERS table = tile columns)
#    clearing: chapters/ch1/clearing.gd
# =====================================================================


# ---------------------------------------------------------------------
#  PART 1: THE FOREST (side view platformer)
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------

# TRIGGER: the chapter starts. A blank sheet of paper, camera zoomed in.
=== intro ===
Y: Tell me something.
  sv: Berätta något.
O: Something. Hmm. I used to know a great many somethings.
  sv: Något. Hmm. Förr kunde jag en hel massa någonting.
Y: Used to?
  sv: Förr?
O: They wander off, somethings. Like cats.
  sv: De vandrar iväg, alla någonting. Som katter.
Y: Then catch one. Quick, before it wanders.
  sv: Fånga ett då. Fort, innan det vandrar iväg.
# (O's attention drifts to something nearby. Keep it vague: we don't say what or where.)
@pause 0.8
O: ...What about him? The fuzzy one. Over there.
  sv: ...Vad sägs om honom? Den luddiga. Där borta.
Y: That's Blobb!
  sv: Det är Blobb!
O: Blobb. Yes. I believe I know half of his story.
  sv: Blobb. Ja. Jag tror att jag kan halva hans saga.
O: You'll have to tell the other half. It's in there somewhere, I'm sure.
  sv: Den andra halvan får du berätta. Den finns säkert där inne någonstans.
Y: Deal. I'm good at halves.
  sv: Okej. Jag är bra på halvor.
O: Once, in the days when the moss was still young, there was a Blobb.
  sv: En gång, på den tiden när mossan fortfarande var ung, fanns det en Blobb.
  @blobb_appears
O: He was round. And fuzzy. And the most magnificent purple.
  sv: Han var rund. Och luddig. Och alldeles praktfullt lila.
  @blobb_color purple
Y: Purple?! Blobb is GREEN. Everybody knows that.
  sv: Lila?! Blobb är GRÖN. Det vet ju alla.
  @blobb_color green
O: Green. There, you see? That was your half already.
  sv: Grön. Där ser du. Det där var redan din halva.
Y: Where does he live?
  sv: Var bor han?
O: In a deep, dark forest, where the trees are so old they've forgotten what the sun looks like...
  sv: I en djup, mörk skog, där träden är så gamla att de har glömt hur solen ser ut...
  @forest_dark
Y: Boring. And scary. Make it a forest someone drew. With crayons. And a big sun.
  sv: Tråkigt. Och läskigt. Gör den till en skog som någon har ritat. Med kritor. Och en stor sol.
  @forest_crayon
O: Crayons, and a big sun. The trees didn't mind one bit.
  sv: Kritor, och en stor sol. Träden hade inget emot det alls.
Y: Better.
  sv: Bättre.
O: And one morning, Blobb set off to see what was on the other side of it all.
  sv: Och en morgon gav sig Blobb iväg för att se vad som fanns på andra sidan alltihop.
# (hint appears: arrows to move)


# TRIGGER: Blobb reaches the first step, which is too high for his little hop (column 19).
=== jump ===
Y: He's stuck.
  sv: Han sitter fast.
O: Blobb's legs were short. Jumping was not among his talents.
  sv: Blobbs ben var korta. Att hoppa hörde inte till hans talanger.
Y: Wrong half. In my half, Blobb jumps higher than trees.
  sv: Fel halva. I min halva hoppar Blobb högre än träd.
  @jump_unlock
O: ...Higher than trees. Well. Your half is louder than mine.
  sv: ...Högre än träd. Nåväl. Din halva hörs mer än min.
# (hint appears: Space / A to jump)


# TRIGGER: just before the first pit (column 31).
=== careful (ambient) ===
Y: Hole! There's a hole!
  sv: Hål! Det är ett hål!
O: Blobb saw it. Blobb had very good eyes for holes.
  sv: Blobb såg det. Blobb hade väldigt bra ögon för hål.


# TRIGGER: Blobb falls into a pit. The scene rewinds to the last checkpoint.
# The game cycles fail_1 to fail_4, then starts over.
=== fail_1 ===
Y: Nope. Blobb didn't fall in. Take it back.
  sv: Nej. Blobb ramlade inte ner. Ta tillbaka det.
O: Taken back. Now, where were we?
  sv: Tillbakataget. Nå, var var vi?

=== fail_2 ===
Y: That's not how it goes.
  sv: Det är inte så det går.
O: No? How does it go?
  sv: Inte? Hur går det då?
Y: He jumps over. Obviously.
  sv: Han hoppar över. Självklart.

=== fail_3 ===
Y: Again!
  sv: Igen!
O: Again.
  sv: Igen.

=== fail_4 ===
Y: Blobbs don't fall. Blobbs bounce.
  sv: Blobbar ramlar inte. Blobbar studsar.
O: I'll write that down somewhere.
  sv: Det ska jag skriva upp någonstans.


# TRIGGER: the open path before the banana (column 64).
=== banana ===
O: But in the middle of the path, there stood a monster.
  sv: Men mitt på stigen stod ett monster.
  @banana_appear
  @hold 0.6
Y: What kind?
  sv: Vad för sort?
O: A banana. An enormous, grumpy banana, as old as the hills.
  sv: En banan. En enorm, sur banan, gammal som gatan.
Y: Bananas can't be grumpy.
  sv: Bananer kan inte vara sura.
O: This one had stood on that path for a very long time. It had earned a little grumpiness.
  sv: Den här hade stått på den där stigen väldigt länge. Den hade förtjänat att vara lite sur.
Y: Nope. Tiny banana. Normal banana size.
  sv: Nix. Pytteliten banan. Vanlig banan-storlek.
  @banana_shrink
  @hold 0.7
O: ...A tiny banana. Still grumpy, though. You can't take that away from it.
  sv: ...En pytteliten banan. Fortfarande sur, dock. Det kan man inte ta ifrån den.


# TRIGGER: the tiny banana bumps into Blobb for the first time.
=== banana_bump (ambient) ===
Y: Hey! No pushing!
  sv: Hallå! Ingen knuffning!
O: Small and stubborn. Perhaps Blobb could simply hop over it.
  sv: Liten och envis. Blobb kunde kanske helt enkelt hoppa över den.


# TRIGGER: Blobb reaches the cliff with the big tree (column 79).
=== branches ===
Y: Way too high.
  sv: Alldeles för högt.
O: It was. Blobb sat down under the old tree and had a long think.
  sv: Det var det. Blobb satte sig under det gamla trädet och tänkte länge.
Y: Then the tree grew branches. For climbing. Right now.
  sv: Då växte det grenar på trädet. Att klättra på. Nu direkt.
  @grow_branches
  @hold 0.8
O: It takes a tree a hundred years to do that.
  sv: Det tar hundra år för ett träd att göra så.
Y: This one was in a hurry.
  sv: Det här hade bråttom.
O: ...Fair enough.
  sv: ...Det kan jag inte säga emot.


# TRIGGER: past the cliff, on the way down (column 100).
=== evening (ambient) ===
Y: Is it getting dark?
  sv: Blir det mörkt nu?
O: The sun was going down, and everything went orange and gold.
  sv: Solen gick ner, och allt blev orange och guld.
  @evening
Y: Orange is the best one.
  sv: Orange är den bästa.
O: Mine used to be blue. I think you might be right, though.
  sv: Min brukade vara blå. Men jag tror att du kanske har rätt.


# TRIGGER: the end of the forest path (column 133).
=== end ===
O: At the end of the path, Blobb found a clearing.
  sv: Vid slutet av stigen hittade Blobb en glänta.
Y: What's a clearing?
  sv: Vad är en glänta?
O: A place where the trees have stepped aside. Only stumps left, and things nobody came back for.
  sv: Ett ställe där träden har klivit undan. Bara stubbar kvar, och saker som ingen kom tillbaka och hämtade.
Y: Show it from above. Like a bird.
  sv: Visa den uppifrån. Som en fågel.
O: Like a bird, then. Up we go.
  sv: Som en fågel, då. Upp med oss.
# (the camera flies up, everything turns white, and we switch to the top-down clearing)


# ---------------------------------------------------------------------
#  PART 2: THE CLEARING (top-down box pushing)
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------

# TRIGGER: the clearing scene starts (room 1).
=== room1 ===
O: From up high, the clearing looked just like a puzzle.
  sv: Uppifrån såg gläntan ut precis som ett pussel.
Y: What does Blobb look like from up here?
  sv: Hur ser Blobb ut härifrån?
O: Like a fuzzy pancake.
  sv: Som en luddig pannkaka.
  @pancake
  @hold 0.4
Y: A PANCAKE!
  sv: En PANNKAKA!
O: Old wooden crates lay everywhere. And the way out was shut tight.
  sv: Gamla trälådor låg överallt. Och vägen ut var stängd.
Y: Who leaves boxes in a forest?
  sv: Vem lämnar lådor i en skog?
O: Someone who was moving, perhaps.
  sv: Någon som skulle flytta, kanske.
Y: Moving where?
  sv: Flytta vart?
O: Somewhere else. If Blobb pushes the crates onto the flat stones, perhaps the gate will remember how to open.
  sv: Någon annanstans. Om Blobb knuffar lådorna till de platta stenarna kanske grinden kommer ihåg hur man öppnar sig.
# (hint appears: arrows, Z undo, R restart)


# TRIGGER: every stone has a crate on it for the first time.
=== gate_open (ambient) ===
Y: It remembered!
  sv: Den kom ihåg!


# TRIGGER: the player restarts a room (R) for the first time.
=== restart (ambient) ===
Y: Do that bit again.
  sv: Gör om den biten.
O: From the top.
  sv: Från början.


# TRIGGER: Blobb walks through the gate into room 2.
=== room2 ===
O: Behind the gate, a little stream ran right through the clearing.
  sv: Bakom grinden rann en liten bäck rakt genom gläntan.
Y: Can Blobb swim?
  sv: Kan Blobb simma?
O: Not yet.
  sv: Inte än.


# TRIGGER: Blobb stands next to the water, or tries to push a crate into it.
# Until this scene has played, crates can't go in the water.
=== float ===
Y: Wait. Do boxes float?
  sv: Vänta. Flyter lådor?
O: Wooden ones do, I believe. Push one in, and maybe it'll hold him up.
  sv: Trälådor gör det, tror jag. Knuffa i en, så kanske den bär honom.
  @crates_float
Y: Box bridge!
  sv: Lådbro!


# TRIGGER: Blobb leaves room 2. The music fades, Blobb lies down, the light turns blue.
# Keep this ambiguous: nothing here may suggest WHICH of them is unwell, or where they are.
=== ending ===
O: On the other side, the grass was warm and soft. So Blobb lay down to rest.
  sv: På andra sidan var gräset varmt och mjukt. Så Blobb lade sig ner och vilade.
Y: Is that the end?
  sv: Är det slut nu?
O: The end of this part. The rest can keep until tomorrow.
  sv: Slut på den här delen. Resten kan vänta till i morgon.
Y: Will you be here tomorrow?
  sv: Är du här i morgon?
  @fade_to_black
@pause 1.6
O: ...Where else would I be?
  sv: ...Var skulle jag annars vara?
Y: Then tomorrow, Blobb learns to swim.
  sv: I morgon lär sig Blobb simma, då.
O: Tomorrow, Blobb learns to swim.
  sv: I morgon lär sig Blobb simma.
# (silence... one quiet beep from a monitor... then the chapter card)


# =====================================================================

# ---------------------------------------------------------------------

#  @blobb_color X      recolours Blobb: purple | green
#  @forest_dark        a dark, misty, realistic forest fades in
#  @forest_crayon      the forest turns into a crayon drawing
#  @jump_unlock        Blobb can now jump high
#  @banana_appear      the giant banana rises up and blocks the path
#  @banana_shrink      the banana shrinks to normal banana size

#  @evening            everything turns orange and gold
