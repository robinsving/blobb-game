#  Rules (docs/story.md): they are EQUALS building a story half each. Never parent/child:
#  no family words, no bedtime or caretaker talk, nobody asks the other for permission,
#  nobody reassures or grants. Never hint WHICH of them is unwell: each crack in one voice
#  is balanced by one in the other (here: O loses the thread in "current", Y loses their
#  words in "ending"). Never mention drawing, crayons, paper or pencils. Blobb is "he".
#  Setting: outdoors (a river and an old mill's pond), never anything that feels indoor
#  or institutional. Hints this chapter: a drip all through it, two beeps at the end.

# ---------------------------------------------------------------------
#  PART 1: THE MILLPOND (side view, under water)
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------

# TRIGGER: the chapter starts. Blobb on the bank of a dark, murky pond.
=== intro ===
O: Now. Where had we got to?
  sv: Nå. Var var vi någonstans?
Y: Swimming. Blobb learns to swim. That was the deal.
  sv: Simma. Blobb lär sig simma. Det var det vi sa.
O: So it was. The stream from the clearing ran downhill, and Blobb followed it all the way to a millpond.
  sv: Just det. Bäcken från gläntan rann nedåt, och Blobb följde den hela vägen till en kvarndamm.
O: The water was as brown as old tea. You couldn't see your own feet in it.
  sv: Vattnet var brunt som gammalt te. Man kunde inte se sina egna fötter i det.
Y: Yuck. Make it clear. And sunny. With little fish.
  sv: Usch. Gör vattnet klart. Och soligt. Med små fiskar.
  @pond_clear
O: Clear as glass, then. And the sun came down in long stripes.
  sv: Klart som glas, då. Och solen sken ner i långa strimmor.
Y: Better.
  sv: Bättre.
# (hint appears: walk right to jump in)


# TRIGGER: Blobb drops into the water for the first time.
=== splash ===
O: In jumped Blobb, with a splash... and down he went, like a stone.
  sv: Plask, sa det när Blobb hoppade i... och så sjönk han som en sten.
  @sink
Y: Why?!
  sv: Varför?!
O: Fuzz. It drinks up water like a sponge. He had never been so heavy.
  sv: Luddet. Det suger upp vatten som en tvättsvamp. Han hade aldrig varit så tung.
Y: Then he walks. Along the bottom. Like a crab.
  sv: Då går han. Längs botten. Som en krabba.
O: Like a very determined crab.
  sv: Som en mycket bestämd krabba.
# (hint appears: walk and hop)


# TRIGGER: the spring bubbling up from the riverbed (column 27).
=== bubbles ===
Y: Wait. Fuzz holds bubbles. Everybody knows that.
  sv: Vänta. Ludd är fullt av bubblor. Det vet ju alla.
O: Does it?
  sv: Gör det?
Y: Loads. Blobb's full of bubbles now. He floats.
  sv: Massor. Blobb är full av bubblor nu. Han flyter.
  @swim_unlock
O: ...And so he did. Up he came, light as a cork.
  sv: ...Och det gjorde han. Upp åkte han, lätt som en kork.
# (hint appears: arrows swim every way)


# TRIGGER: just past the big rock (column 35).
=== cold (ambient) ===
O: Pond water is cold, even in summer. Blobb went quite blue.
  sv: Vattnet i en damm är kallt, även på sommaren. Blobb blev alldeles blå.
  @blobb_blue
Y: Blue's fine. Blue's a swimming colour.
  sv: Blått är bra. Blått är en simfärg.


# TRIGGER: under the warden's stump (column 40).
=== lifeguard (ambient) ===
O: On an old stump sat the pond warden. A small banana, with a whistle.
  sv: På en gammal stubbe satt dammvakten. En liten banan, med en visselpipa.
  @whistle
Y: The grumpy banana! He got a job!
  sv: Den sura bananen! Han har fått jobb!
O: "No running," said the banana.
  sv: "Spring inte här", sa bananen.
  @whistle
Y: He's SWIMMING.
  sv: Han SIMMAR ju.


# TRIGGER: the fish (column 45).
=== fish (ambient) ===
Y: Fish!
  sv: Fiskar!
O: River fish. They don't think much of visitors.
  sv: Flodfiskar. De är inte så förtjusta i besök.


# TRIGGER: where the river starts pushing back (column 57).
# O loses the thread here. Y's matching crack is in "ending".
=== current (ambient) ===
O: Then the river started pushing. Blobb pushed back, harder and harder, and...
  sv: Sen började strömmen trycka på. Blobb tryckte emot, hårdare och hårdare, och...
@pause 1.6
O: ...Where was he?
  sv: ...Var var han?
Y: Near the bottom. The push is weaker down there.
  sv: Nära botten. Strömmen är svagare där nere.
O: Near the bottom. Yes. Thank you.
  sv: Nära botten. Ja. Tack.


# TRIGGER: just before the whirlpool (column 66).
=== whirlpool (ambient) ===
Y: What's that swirly thing?
  sv: Vad är det där snurriga?
O: A whirlpool. It spins anything that comes too close. Best keep clear.
  sv: En virvel. Den snurrar runt allt som kommer för nära. Bäst att hålla sig undan.


# TRIGGER: past the big whirlpool, a short passage of smaller ones (column 77).
=== whirls (ambient) ===
Y: More! A whole row of them!
  sv: Fler! En hel rad!
O: A whole row of little whirlpools, each spinning its own way. Over one, under the next, between two.
  sv: En hel rad små virvlar, som snurrade åt var sitt håll. Över en, under nästa, mellan två.


# TRIGGER: the whirlpool catches Blobb. The scene rewinds to the last checkpoint.
# The game cycles fail_1 to fail_4, then starts over.
=== fail_1 ===
Y: Nope. He swam right round it.
  sv: Nix. Han simmade förbi den.
O: Right round it. Of course he did.
  sv: Förbi den. Så klart.

=== fail_2 ===
Y: That's not what happened.
  sv: Det var inte så det hände.
O: What did happen?
  sv: Vad hände då?
Y: He did a loop-the-loop. Then he went round it.
  sv: Han gjorde en kullerbytta. Sen simmade han förbi.

=== fail_3 ===
Y: Again!
  sv: Igen!
O: Again.
  sv: Igen.

=== fail_4 ===
Y: Whirlpools can't catch Blobbs. Too fuzzy.
  sv: Virvlar kan inte fånga Blobbar. För luddiga.
O: Too fuzzy. I'll try to keep that in mind.
  sv: För luddiga. Det ska jag försöka komma ihåg.


# TRIGGER: the far end of the pond (column 115). Then we cut to the mill channel.
=== deep_end ===
O: At the far end of the pond stood the old mill, and under it the water ran in through a dark channel.
  sv: Längst bort i dammen stod den gamla kvarnen, och under den rann vattnet in i en mörk kanal.
  @gurgle
Y: What's that noise?
  sv: Vad är det för ljud?
O: Something stuck in there, going round and round. Something yellow, and very cross.
  sv: Något har fastnat där inne och snurrar runt, runt. Något gult, och väldigt argt.


# ---------------------------------------------------------------------
#  PART 2: THE MILL CHANNEL (move the water, the water moves the duck)
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------

# TRIGGER: the mill scene starts (room 1). A rubber duck behind the fence.
=== room1 ===
Y: A duck! A rubber duck!
  sv: En anka! En badanka!
O: A rubber duck, stuck behind the mill's old fence. And the warden would not stop whistling about it.
  sv: En badanka, fast bakom kvarnens gamla staket. Och vakten bara visslade och visslade.
  @whistle
Y: Blobb can't get through the fence.
  sv: Blobb kommer inte igenom staketet.
O: No. But water can. The wheels open the spouts, and the levers turn the boards.
  sv: Nej. Men vattnet kommer igenom. Hjulen sätter på vattnet, och spakarna vänder brädorna.
Y: So Blobb moves the water, and the water moves the duck.
  sv: Så Blobb flyttar vattnet, och vattnet flyttar ankan.
# (hint appears: swim to a wheel or lever, Space to use it, R to start the room again)


# TRIGGER: room 1 is solved. The duck pops up out of the water.
=== duck_out ===
O: Up went the duck, out of the water, into the air...
  sv: Upp flög ankan, ur vattnet, rakt upp i luften...
Y: ...and onto the banana's head!
  sv: ...och ner på bananens huvud!
  @duck_bonk
O: The banana threw it straight back in.
  sv: Bananen kastade genast tillbaka den.
  @duck_back
Y: BANANA!
  sv: BANAN!
O: Grumpiness is catching.
  sv: Det smittar, det där med att vara sur.


# TRIGGER: room 2 starts.
=== room2 ===
O: This time it landed deeper in, where three spouts all argue with each other.
  sv: Den här gången landade den längre in, där tre vattenstrålar grälar med varandra.
Y: Blobb's not scared of arguing.
  sv: Blobb är inte rädd för gräl.


# TRIGGER: room 3 starts. The way out is a round hatch.
=== room3 ===
O: The last bit. Behind the mill, a round hatch led out to the shallow end.
  sv: Sista biten. Bakom kvarnen ledde en rund lucka ut till den grunda delen.
Y: The duck wants out. So does Blobb.
  sv: Ankan vill ut. Det vill Blobb också.


# TRIGGER: in room 3, after a while (or a few presses). Until this plays, room 3 can't be solved.
=== backwards ===
Y: This is impossible. The water goes the wrong way.
  sv: Det här är omöjligt. Vattnet rinner åt fel håll.
O: Water only goes one way. Downhill.
  sv: Vatten rinner bara åt ett håll. Nedåt.
Y: Not in my half. In my half, it can go backwards.
  sv: Inte i min halva. I min halva kan det rinna baklänges.
  @reverse
O: ...Backwards. Well. Turn a wheel twice and see what it does.
  sv: ...Baklänges. Jaha. Vrid ett hjul två gånger och se vad som händer.
# (hint appears: a wheel goes off, on, backwards)


# TRIGGER: room 3 is solved.
=== hatch ===
O: Through the hatch went the duck. And Blobb, right behind it.
  sv: Ut genom luckan åkte ankan. Och Blobb tätt efter.


# TRIGGER: the duck can't get out from here any more (no solution left). The room starts over after it.
=== stuck (ambient) ===
O: The duck is stuck.
  sv: Ankan har fastnat.
Y: Then we do it again.
  sv: Då gör vi om det.


# TRIGGER: the player restarts a room (R) for the first time.
=== restart (ambient) ===
Y: Start that bit again.
  sv: Börja om den biten.
O: From the top.
  sv: Från början.


# TRIGGER: through the hatch. Music fades, warm light, a drip.
# Keep this ambiguous: nothing may suggest WHICH of them is unwell, or where they are.
=== ending ===
O: Through the hatch was the shallow end, where the sun had warmed the water all day.
  sv: På andra sidan luckan låg den grunda delen, där solen hade värmt vattnet hela dagen.
  @float_back
O: Blobb floated on his back. The duck floated beside him, still cross.
  sv: Blobb flöt på rygg. Ankan flöt bredvid honom, fortfarande arg.
Y: Let's leave them there a while. In the warm bit.
  sv: Vi låter dem vara där en stund. I det varma.
@pause 1.8
Y: ...Wait, where did they get to? I was thinking about bubbles.
  sv: ...Vänta, var hamnade de? Jag tänkte på bubblor.
O: So was I. We were watching the same bubbles.
  sv: Jag med. Vi tittade på samma bubblor.
Y: Where do bubbles go when they pop?
  sv: Vart tar bubblorna vägen när de spricker?
@pause 1.2
O: Up. Into the air. Everywhere, a little.
  sv: Upp. Ut i luften. Lite överallt.
Y: ...Like stars?
  sv: ...Som stjärnor?
O: Maybe. From down here you can't tell them apart.
  sv: Kanske. Härifrån går de inte att skilja åt.
  @fade_to_black
@pause 1.4
Y: Then Blobb watches lights tomorrow. Lots of them. At night, from up high.
  sv: I så fall tittar Blobb på ljus i morgon. Massor av dem. På natten, högt uppifrån.
O: You bring the lights. I'll bring the night.
  sv: Ljusen är din halva. Natten är min.
# (silence... two quiet beeps... then straight on into chapter 3)
